This laboratory activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud computing platforms can support different types of organizations, such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. AWS impressed me the most because of its flexibility and extensive selection of cloud services. I learned that AWS can provide solutions for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other business requirements. AWS, Azure, and GCP are similar because all three provide cloud-based services that organizations can use without having to manage their own physical infrastructure. They also offer services such as virtual machines, cloud storage, databases, networking, identity and access management, and Kubernetes, and all support scalability, allowing businesses to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs. Business requirements are also important when selecting a cloud platform. Different companies have different budgets, existing technologies, workloads, and future goals. For example, organizations that already depend on Microsoft technologies may prefer Azure because of its compatibility with Microsoft products, while companies that focus heavily on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data may consider GCP. AWS, meanwhile, can be suitable for businesses looking for the broadest selection of services. Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that cloud selection requires careful analysis instead of choosing a provider based only on popularity. I need to consider the client's budget, current infrastructure, workload, security requirements, scalability, and long-term plans.
